AI in Healthcare Hackathon

About

The AI in Healthcare Hackathon is a two-day build event run by the Leeds Artificial Intelligence Society as part of Leeds AI Week. From 8am on Saturday 24 October to 5pm on Sunday 25 October, students from across the UK will meet at HELIX Leeds to turn real clinical problems into working prototypes.

You don’t need to be an ML expert, or a programmer at all. Teams of four mix medical students with computer science and engineering students, because the best healthtech ideas come from people who understand the problem working with people who can build the solution. Apply as a team or on your own, and we’ll match you with others.

The brief is completely open: any healthcare problem, any technology. To get you started, the weekend opens with a talk on clinical challenges in surgery and the patient pathway, followed by hands-on technical workshops. Have a look at the challenge brief for example problem areas and the judging criteria.

Projects are submitted on Devpost by 11:30 on Sunday and pitched to a panel of judges from industry, the NHS and academia. Prizes include first and second place and an award for the Most Responsible Use of Data. Places are limited, so register on Luma to secure yours.
